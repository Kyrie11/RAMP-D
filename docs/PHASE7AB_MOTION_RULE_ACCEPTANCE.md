## Research choices
PRECOG (ICCV 2019) learns joint density from data and conditional goal optimization; Trajectron++ (ECCV 2020) ego-plan conditioned CVAE with dynamics; Forecast-MAE (ICCV 2023) complementary history/future/map masking only in training and removes future input at fine-tuning; TrafficSim (CVPR 2021) unrolls imitation plus common-sense collision regularization. None supplies observed human responses to every alternate action. Use real factual training as core; optional history/map pretraining only if needed; limited model-conditioned interventions for decision-critical behavior, plus independent reachable response support and Teacher. Do not train to erase real unsafe behavior. All eight heads have independent masks; only factual joint is presently active; ranker optional separate diagnostic, event loss unit tested, no other head trained.
