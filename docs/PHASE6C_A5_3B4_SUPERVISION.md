## Waymax response methodology
Official Waymax paper and source; Treiber et al. IDM (Congested Traffic States in Empirical Observations and Microscopic Simulations, 2000), MOBIL lane-change paper; TrafficSim learned-response approach later. IDMRoutePolicy uses logged future route geometry: label as oracle-route conditional baseline, not deployable prediction. Start with official discrete reference parity and physical units; acceleration/curvature are not jerk/steering rate. Reference point, angle wrapping, speed reversal, clipping, friction, timestep and coordinate precision require explicit adapters. Use local-frame arithmetic; compare one-step arc and convergence over multiple steps; zero-speed braking needs a stop-aware chosen model. Use IDM baseline, SUMO route/lane change calibration, then learned multi-agent response if validation justifies. No log playback counterfactual. WOSAC held-out physical/interaction realism for compatible ordinary-road scenes; separate construction stress tests. Paired trials share actor-keyed external random draws and initial history, never force all endogenous outcomes equal. No formal safety truth from learned response or empirical bounds.
