## Three rule classes and physical modeling
External authority request is exogenous, physical deployment can depend on traffic clearance. Planned closure: advance warnings, entry restriction, drain, closed pending confirmed physical deployment, remove pending, confirmed absence then reopen. With missing/incomplete interval measurements, stale commands or failed placement: do not open. Interlock not a proof of sensor correctness. Actual CARLA geometry and collision object registry plus SUMO permissions/queue detectors must share exact tick, resource IDs and ack generation. Sudden obstruction: physical hazard onset independent of legal administrative closure and ego observation; stopped vehicle decelerates from feasible trajectory, falling object has physical insertion/trajectory and collision handling (no teleport into occupied area), separate formal order / message issue / receipt / local detection. Sudden cases may be unavoidable; retain them and report proof scope/UNKNOWN. Do not require drain before physical hazard exists. Traffic-driven formal control: validated occupancy/detector latency, min/conditional max green, queue difference/hysteresis, yellow/all-red and clearance before reversal, no forcing opposite lane if blocked. Informal zipper/negotiation is response, not legal permission. Paired runs freeze exogenous mechanisms, not causal descendants (actual deployment, queues, actuated phase may change).

## 8 supervision sources
Event: authority/event mechanism logs, WZDx verification-aware priors, sampled WOMD signal transitions auxiliary only; phase/event/source/scope and censored times. Joint: factual WOMD & AV2 with identity/history/future mask; observed ego path surrogate, paired responsive simulation for unexecuted control. Occupancy: sampled valid actor footprint vs map-resource geometry; dimensions/reference/height/topology known, ambiguous masked; not continuous proof. Observation: CARLA visibility + detector separate, local rule awareness proxies, distinct message generation/receipt and nonreceipt states; no track-valid proxy. Viability: independently replayed positive certificates, negative only proven complete finite policy class, unknown masked. Margin: conservative per-constraint certified lower bounds, same problem scale, upper bounds/ranking scope explicit; no TTC substitute. Deadline: exact shared prefix and absolute clocks, verified lower/upper bounds; distinguish T_obs^+ from T_sep,+ until paper reconciles. Causal: external invariance of exogenous requests, actuation through flow/controller and paired interventions; CausalAgents optional robustness only. Raw label provenance, masks, unit, scenario/intervention/policy/model/version and evidence hashes retained.

Distribution plot is counts, not realism. Actual later gates: independent geometry test + labeled BEV and sign/occlusion views; rule/event/command log replay and paired A/A reproducibility; A/B response and external request invariance; logged vs simulated kinematics/interaction/map distribution (WOSAC and per-scenario bootstrap stratified by density and junction); independent oracle and unknown/mask coverage; held-out map/event/seed split, no leakage. Score WOSAC on factual ordinary road scenarios separately from synthetic workzone interventions. A high realism metric does not prove causal correctness or safety. Freeze thresholds on validation before held-out evaluation. No actual simulator labels or metrics produced in this round.

RuleNet-main inspection: reference robustness_attention.py and JointLoss.py are for INTERACTION; use architecture ideas only. Its mean prediction minus mean ground-truth violation term is not a calibrated supervised regression loss (target term constant w.r.t. model); dimension and mask checks needed. Do not transplant that loss or safety semantics. Network remains soft ordering/proposal; not the certified verifier.
